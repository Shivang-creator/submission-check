submission-check

Lint a hackathon submission before you send it.

Ten rules, each one learned from a real submission that lost. Every finding quotes an exact span of your draft, gives you one fix, and names the loss it came from.

Load an example

The same ten rules as python cli.py draft.md. No model reads your draft — the findings come from pure functions, which is why every quote is verifiable.

Your draft is posted to the checker, linted, and returned in the response. Nothing is stored.
